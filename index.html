I study the design and operation of sustainable, intelligent, and connected urban mobility systems. My research links network performance with heterogeneous travel behaviour and develops policies for congestion management, information provision, and shared mobility.

Previously, I was a Lecturer and Postdoctoral Researcher at EPFL. I received my PhD in Industrial and Systems Engineering from the National University of Singapore.

Research

Current research

  • Tailored policy interventions: road-space allocation, transit priority, and coordination between public transit and ride-hailing.
  • Demand under uncertainty: route, mode, and scheduling choices under incomplete information and bounded rationality.
  • Shared mobility: matching, participation, and decentralised organisation in ridesharing and autonomous mobility-on-demand.

Methods

  • Game theory and equilibrium analysis
  • Mathematical optimisation
  • Structural estimation and discrete choice
  • Multi-agent simulation and machine learning

Research agenda

My next research programme focuses on scalable on-demand mobility services with autonomous vehicles and the inference of travel preferences from emerging data streams. The goal is to improve the sustainability, scalability, and stability of future mobility systems.
