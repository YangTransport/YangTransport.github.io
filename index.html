I study the design and operation of sustainable, intelligent, and connected urban mobility systems. My research links network performance with heterogeneous travel behaviour and develops policies for congestion management, information provision, and shared mobility.

Research

Current research

  • Tailored mobility policies: bottleneck metering, transit priority zones, road-space allocation, and pricing policies that coordinate travellers, platforms, and public transport.
  • Travel behaviour and information: inference of scheduling preferences from congestion and choice data, with a focus on risk, heterogeneity, and rational inattention.
  • Shared and robotic mobility: matching, participation, and decentralised coordination in ridesharing, autonomous mobility, and vehicle-drone delivery.

Methods

  • Game theory and equilibrium analysis
  • Mathematical optimisation
  • Multi-agent simulation and reinforcement learning

Research agenda

My next research programme focuses on scalable on-demand robotic mobility systems, including autonomous vehicles and delivery robots, and on inferring travel preferences from emerging data streams. The goal is to improve the sustainability, scalability, and stability of future mobility systems.
