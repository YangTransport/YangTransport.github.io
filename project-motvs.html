MOTVS: Human-oriented ubiquitous mobility

This programme studies how shared-mobility systems and public-transport policies can be designed around heterogeneous traveller behaviour.

Research question

How can public agencies and mobility platforms improve system efficiency without overlooking participation and strategic responses?

Approach

I combine equilibrium models, game theory, and optimisation to connect network-level regulation with platform matching and long-term participation.

Contribution

The resulting studies develop transit priority zones that account for ride-hailing competition and ridesharing models with endogenous and flexible user participation.
